# Notebooks — the didactic series

Six notebooks that explain, in order, what three replicated papers claim, what
the replications actually measured, why that motivated a new protocol, where
that new work stands — and a self-contained team tour of the whole arc.

**They are documentation, not research.** No new experiment is run here and no new
finding is produced. Every measured number is a hard-coded constant traceable to
its artefact: the numbers produced by this programme map to lines of
[`../EVIDENCE-INDEX.md`](../EVIDENCE-INDEX.md); inherited paper-audit numbers
(noted inline where they appear) map to
[`../../01-info-propagation/overview/EVIDENCE-INDEX.md`](../../01-info-propagation/overview/EVIDENCE-INDEX.md).

## Running them

Every notebook runs **end to end on a clean checkout with no `data/` and no
`results/`**. Restart kernel, run all; the whole series takes under a minute.

```bash
# once, from the repository root
02-ext-research/.venv/bin/python -m ipykernel install \
    --user --name ext-research --display-name "Python (02-ext-research)"

# regenerate any notebook from its builder
02-ext-research/.venv/bin/python scripts/build_ext_00_notebook.py    # .. _05_
```

Constraints these notebooks are built to satisfy:

- **Measured numbers are constants.** `.gitignore` excludes `results/` at every
  level and the corpora require applications to their authors, so nothing here
  reads `data/` or `results/`.
- **Anything demonstrable on synthetic data is computed live** — the rank-10
  singular spectrum, the regularisation decay, the α-curves and escort
  distributions, the noise-floor simulation, the majority classifier, the
  degenerate graph, the snowflake decode bug.
- **No notebook imports `botsage/`, `dtwre/` or `disinfo/`.** Notebook 04 imports
  `renyiext.spectrum` and `renyiext.generators`, and only those — they depend on
  numpy alone and read no data.
- **`OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` is set before the first import**, per the 133× OpenMP
  scheduling penalty measured in
  [`../bitacora/04_p2_temporal.md`](../bitacora/04_p2_temporal.md) §6.
- **Every accuracy is printed beside its majority baseline**, and every notebook
  ends with "What this licenses" and "What this does NOT license".

## They are build artefacts

Each `.ipynb` is generated by `scripts/build_ext_NN_notebook.py`, sharing
`scripts/_ext_notebook.py` (cell plumbing, palette, the setup cell). **Edit the
builder, never the notebook** — changes to the `.ipynb` are lost on the next
regeneration.

Exporting to PDF needs the patch documented in the repository `CLAUDE.md`: go via
`--to latex`, insert
`\makeatletter\@ifundefined{c@none}{\newcounter{none}}{}\makeatother` before
`\begin{document}`, then run `xelatex` twice.

## What this series does not claim

Stated here as well as in each notebook, because it is the point most likely to
be lost in summary:

1. **The three papers are not the villain.** Two of three replications reproduce
   their headline numbers closely. The finding is that the published
   *comparisons* do not isolate what they claim to isolate.
2. **Nothing here is about AI-generated text.** Every corpus is 2015–2022 and
   carries no ground truth for machine-generated text — an explicit non-goal
   (`../docs/00-CHARTER.md` §4).
3. **The new work's own floor was not cleared.** Notebook 04 reports the
   burstiness floor failure and the unsupported mechanism at the same weight as
   the passing hypothesis.
